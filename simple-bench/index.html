Scores

What this means for the reader

Scores: Jon's PC (Ryzen 7 2700X, GTX 1050 Ti, Chrome 155) = 1000; higher is faster. The suggestion uses the Book reader's own rules (its rules.js, copied in by build.py), so it matches the reader's first-run check. "Runs well" = 1.3x real time after the model's margin; "barely" = keeps up with little to spare; phones, tablets and laptops on battery count 0.8 of their speed for warming up. The reader's full check also measures memory, which this short test does not.
