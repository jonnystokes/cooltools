# Textures: where they come from

Ground textures from Poly Haven, all CC0 (public domain: free to use, change and share, no credit required;
credited here anyway). Fetched 2026-11-15 by the lead at 1K (color.webp, normal.webp, rough.webp) and 2K (the same names with -2k), converted to WebP: color, normal (OpenGL), roughness.
`index.json` lists each set's maps, what it is for and its real size in meters where Poly Haven gives one.
